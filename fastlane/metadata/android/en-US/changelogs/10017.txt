Your pairing secret no longer travels over the network. Each request is signed
with it instead, so anyone watching learns nothing they can reuse, and a
captured request cannot be replayed. Your computer signs its replies too, so
the app can tell it apart from anything else answering.

Requests from outside your local network are now refused outright.

The pairing code can be replaced from the setup command, and on the phone the
secret now lives in the Android keystore.
