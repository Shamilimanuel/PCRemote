A new row of tools under each PC:

- Timers: shut down when a download finishes, or on a schedule.
- Media: volume, mute, play, pause and skip.
- Apps: start a game or program you chose on the PC.
- Message: pop a note up on the PC's screen.
- Screen: see the PC's screen, if allowed on the PC.
- Activity: what was done, when, and by which phone.

Graphics card use now shows too. Update the PC as well: type reveille in
PowerShell, then Maintenance › Update.
